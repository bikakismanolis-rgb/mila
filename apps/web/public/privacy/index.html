Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Mila is a messenger that uses your email address instead of your phone number. This page explains what we hold, why we hold it, and how you get rid of it. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

Your messages are not end-to-end encrypted. They are stored on our database server as ordinary text. That means we are technically able to read them, and so is anyone who obtains access to the database. We do not read them as a matter of practice. The one exception is described under “Reports” below. We will not pretend to a protection we have not built. Do not use Mila for anything that would harm you if it were exposed.

Who is responsible

The data controller is [COMPANY NAME], [REGISTERED ADDRESS], [COUNTRY]. For anything in this policy, or to exercise any of the rights below, write to [CONTACT EMAIL]. We answer within 30 days, as the GDPR requires.

What we collect

Your email address How you sign in, and how other people find you. Required. Other users see it only after you have accepted a conversation with them, and you can hide it from everyone in settings. Searching by part of an address is not possible; someone has to type the whole address.
Your password Stored only as a cryptographic hash by our authentication provider. We never see it. Not collected if you sign in with Google.
Display name, username, bio Whatever you choose to put on your profile. All optional except the name.
Your messages and attachments The content of what you send, the reactions people add, and which group conversations you are in. Stored until the sender deletes a message “for everyone”, which erases its text and files from our servers. “Delete for me” only hides a message from your own view.
Delivery and read receipts When a message reached someone and when they opened it. You can turn read receipts off in settings.
Contact matching (only if you ask for it) If you upload a contact list to find people you know, your device hashes the email addresses before sending them. We never receive the addresses themselves and we do not store the hashes after matching.
Notification address (only if you turn notifications on) A delivery address for each device, issued by your browser’s push service, plus the keys needed to encrypt notifications for that device. Removed when you turn notifications off, sign out, or delete your account.
Blocks and reports Who you blocked, and any abuse reports you filed or that named you.
Moderation records If your account is suspended for breaking the terms of use: when it happened and a short note saying why.

We do not collect your phone number, your contacts list, your location, or your browsing activity, and no device identifiers beyond the notification address described above. There is no advertising in Mila and no analytics or tracking code. Profile pictures are drawn on your device from your initials, and the app’s fonts are served by us, so opening Mila does not tell any font or avatar service who you are.

Why we are allowed to hold it

  • To provide the service you asked for (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)): your account, your profile, your conversations. Without this there is no app.
  • Our legitimate interest in keeping the service safe (Article 6(1)(f)): blocks, abuse reports, limits on how much an account can send, and account suspensions exist so that people can use Mila without being harassed or spammed.
  • Your consent (Article 6(1)(a)): contact matching only runs when you explicitly start it, and you can withdraw at any time by not running it again.

Reports

If you report someone, we receive the account you reported and the reason you give. The person you reported is not told who reported them. To decide what to do, we may read the conversation between you and that account. That is the only situation in which we read messages.

If an account breaks our terms, we can delete its messages and suspend it. If we find content that the law requires us to report, such as child sexual abuse material, we report it to the authorities.

Who else touches your data

We are a small operation and we do not run our own infrastructure. These companies process data on our behalf, under contract, and are not allowed to use it for their own purposes.

Supabase Database, authentication and file storage. Your account, profile, messages and attachments live here. Region: Frankfurt, Germany (EU).
Vercel Serves the web app. Sees your IP address as any web server would.
Resend Sends account confirmation and password reset emails. Receives your email address.
Google When we offer Google Sign-In, the button on the sign-in page is loaded from Google, so Google sees your IP address and browser when that page opens. If you choose to sign in with Google, Google also confirms your identity to us and we receive your email address and name.
Your browser’s push service (Google, Apple, Mozilla or Microsoft) Only if you turn notifications on. Notifications travel through the service built into your browser or phone. The content is encrypted for your device before it leaves us, so the service can see that a notification was sent and when, but not what it says.

How long we keep it

Your account and profile stay until you delete them. Messages stay until the person who sent them deletes them for everyone. Abuse reports are kept after account deletion, without identifying details, because a safety record that vanishes when the reported person leaves is not a safety record. A suspended account stays suspended until we lift the suspension or the account is deleted.

Deleting your account

Settings → Your account → Delete my account. It takes effect immediately and cannot be undone.

What is erased

  • Your login, password and Google connection.
  • Your email address. It is released and someone else could register it.
  • Your display name, username and bio.
  • Your block list, your reactions, and your notification addresses.
  • Your membership of any group conversations.

What is not

Messages you already sent stay in the recipient's conversation, shown as coming from a deleted user with no name or address attached. We keep them because that conversation is also the other person's record, and deleting one side of it would take away something that is theirs as much as yours.

If you want your sent messages destroyed as well, write to us and say so. That is your right under GDPR Article 17 and we will do it by hand.

Your rights

If you are in the EU or the UK, the GDPR gives you the following. You can exercise the first two yourself, in the app, right now.

  • Access and portability: Settings → Your account → Download my data gives you everything we hold about you as a JSON file.
  • Erasure: the delete button above, or write to us for the harder version.
  • Rectification: edit your profile, or ask us.
  • Objection and restriction: you can object to processing based on our legitimate interest.
  • Complaint: you can complain to your national data protection authority. In Greece this is the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (dpa.gr). You do not need to go through us first.

Children

Mila is not for anyone under 16. We do not knowingly hold data about children. If you believe a child has an account, tell us and we will remove it.

Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit. Access to the database is governed by row-level rules so that one account cannot read another's conversations, and those rules are tested automatically before every change. Passwords are hashed. Accounts are limited in how many messages, requests and files they can send, which slows down spam. As stated at the top, message content is not encrypted at rest, which is the most important limitation on this page.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will say so in the app before the change takes effect, not quietly edit this page. The date at the top always reflects the current version.
